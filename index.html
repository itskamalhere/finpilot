Privacy & Architecture

100% Client-Side & Private: FinPilot operates entirely in your local web browser. Your financial numbers, transactions, notes, and budgets are never transmitted to external servers, cloud services, or third parties.

Local Storage Notice: Data persists in your browser's local storage (keys: finpilot_transactions_v1, finpilot_budgets_v1, finpilot_settings_v1). Clearing your browser cookies and site data will delete your locally stored records. Make regular CSV backups to preserve your history.

Security Notice: FinPilot does not collect banking credentials, passwords, or payment cards. It is an offline personal budgeting tool and is not a banking institution.
